# Hi, I'm Anurag

I finished my M.S. in Statistical Data Science at San Francisco State University in May 2026. I work on machine learning for medical diagnostics and on statistics in the age of AI, and I build things on the side.

## Medical diagnostics

Each of these has a demo that runs in the browser.

- [Malaria cell classifier](https://github.com/nepalanurag/Detection-of-Malaria-Using-CNN) — CNN on blood smear images. 93.2% accuracy, AUC 0.958. [Live demo](https://malaria-cnn-web.vercel.app)
- [COVID CT infection mapping](https://github.com/nepalanurag/Biomedical-Imaging-Analysis) — segmentation and quantification of COVID lung infection in CT scans. [Live demo](https://covid-ct-web.vercel.app)
- [ECG heartbeat classifier](https://github.com/nepalanurag/ECG-Classification) — arrhythmia classification at 98.8% accuracy on the shipped split, 87% under strict patient-grouped cross-validation. [Live demo](https://ecg-classification-web.vercel.app)

## Statistics meets AI

- [LLM annotator reliability](https://github.com/nepalanurag/llm-annotator-reliability) — can an LLM replace human annotators? 248 abstracts, 96.0% accuracy, kappa 0.946.
- [RAG evaluation lab](https://github.com/nepalanurag/rag-eval-lab) — what actually moves RAG answer quality. Honest null result: no factor reached significance.
- [Synthetic data tradeoffs](https://github.com/nepalanurag/synthetic-data-tradeoffs) — fidelity, utility, and privacy of synthetic tabular data.
- [Double machine learning](https://github.com/nepalanurag/causal-double-ml) — causal effect estimation on 401(k) data against a known simulated truth.

## Thesis

- [Permutation-assisted group lasso](https://github.com/nepalanurag/thesis-findings) — feature selection for multi-modal data: every feature has to beat its own permuted decoys to get selected. Validated on TCGA breast cancer data with selection stability Jaccard 0.680 and AUC 0.948. The repo includes the simulation engine under `code/`. [Results site](https://anurag-thesis.vercel.app)
